Flood potential zones from the ensemble maximum unit streamflow of EF5 (m3/s per km2): View A shows the hazard alone, View B crosses the ensemble likelihood with the exposure of the built-up areas. Two band sets: the FLASH set (0.5, 1, 2, 4) for comparability with the Guatemala product, and a steep island set (2, 4, 6, 10) that keeps the pattern readable on the volcanic islands. Raster layers add the probability, the rainfall analysis and forecast, and the ensemble percentiles of the featured cycles.

Zones are ranked by class, confidence and area; a dashed border marks low confidence (5 to 20 percent of members above the first band). The zoom buttons also select the island of the rainfall chart. Cycles marked * carry the ensemble percentile rasters. The basemap needs a connection; the product layers work offline.

Featured cycle: the EF5 percentile rasters of the 50 members and of the 10 analysis members are available in the raster menu.

Columns: cumulative QPE analysis to the cycle (last hour) in mm, island mean; 24 h forecast rain median (p05 to p95) in mm, island mean; area with p90 unit streamflow above the second band, km2; worst zone class or level; population in View B zones at Moderate or worse.

Zones: cells where at least 3 of 50 members exceed the first band, closed and opened morphologically, 8 connected, at least 0.32 km2, holes filled. Class: highest band reached by the 90th percentile of the members inside the zone (the median and the worst member are in the popup). View B: likelihood tier (5, 20, 50 percent) crossed with impact, where impact is the potential class raised by one where the built density is in the upper tercile or a facility lies within 500 m, and lowered by one where no building lies nearby. Population, buildings and facilities inside each zone come from the Overture Maps receptor base with the 2017 census allocation. Confidence * = low. Use the arrow keys to step through cycles.
